Get help · An honest list

I can't fix this for you. But I won't send you off with nothing.

This site is a place to be heard — not a legal service, a shelter, or a fund. If you're in the middle of it right now, these are the people who actually do the helping. Call them before you take my word for anything.

If it's urgent

Facing eviction or losing your voucher

  • Oregon Law Center

    Free civil legal aid · Medford office serves Jackson County

    Free legal help for low-income tenants facing eviction, voucher termination, or housing discrimination. Ask about their intake process. oregonlawcenter.org

  • Community Alliance of Tenants — Renters' Rights Hotline

    Statewide Oregon tenant hotline

    Peer counselors who can explain your rights around notices, evictions, and repairs. oregoncat.org

  • Fair Housing Council of Oregon

    Housing discrimination

    If you believe you were treated differently because of family status, disability, race, or another protected class. fhco.org

Housing & basic needs

Local, Jackson County

  • 211info

    Dial 2-1-1 · around the clock

    The front door to local help — shelter beds, rent assistance, food, and family services. Tell them you have a child in the household; ask specifically what family options exist. 211info.org

  • ACCESS

    Jackson County community action agency

    Rent and energy assistance, housing programs, and food for Jackson County residents. accesshelps.org

  • Housing Authority of Jackson County (HAJC)

    2251 Table Rock Road, Medford · 541-779-5785

    Administers Housing Choice Vouchers locally. If your assistance is being terminated, you generally have the right to request an informal hearing — read your letter's deadline carefully and don't miss it. hajc.net

Please double-check before you rely on this

Phone numbers, offices, and programs change. Treat this as a starting list, not gospel — confirm current details directly with each organization. And if you're up against a written deadline on an eviction or a voucher termination, talk to a lawyer as early as you can. Nothing on this page is legal advice.
