Add your voice

How to tell your story here.

There's no form on this page on purpose. Your words shouldn't have to squeeze through a text box with a character limit. You email it, and it goes up the way you wrote it.

1 · Write it out

Any length. Your words. Facts are welcome but never required — this is your account, not a case you have to win. Say what happened and how it hit you.

2 · Email it

Send it to the address below. Tell us the name (or first name, or “anonymous”) and the town you want shown. Nothing else is published about you.

3 · It goes up as written

A light proofread for typos, and that's it. No edits to what you said, no comments allowed underneath, no uploads to manage. What you send is what appears.

voices@rvrentersvoices.org →

A few honest limits: this isn't legal advice or a legal service, and posting here doesn't start or help a case. Please don't send anything you couldn't stand behind, and — for your own sake and others' — write about your own experience rather than accusing named people of crimes. If you need real help, start with Get Help.
