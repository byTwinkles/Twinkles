## HUD VOUCHER TERMINATION + 3-YEAR BAR (DOCUMENTED — IMG_3711, the termination letter)
**HAJC = Housing Authority of Jackson County**, 2251 Table Rock Road, Medford OR 97501,
541-779-5785, hajc.net. Letter dated **June 15, 2026**, to Tracey Kelly, from
**Christine Sampson, HCV Case Coordinator** (christine@hajc.net, ext. 1011).

- "Your Landlord, Pacific Properties was granted restitution of their property located at
  747 Highland Dr and you were ordered to vacate by **May 22, 2026**." ⚠️ The letter's **May 22**
  date matches NO court document — every court doc says **May 31, 2026.** (Tracey formally disputes this.)
- "**Effective May 31, 2026, your housing assistance is terminated**" for violation of Family Obligations.
- Authority cited: **HAJC Administrative Plan 12-I.D. Mandatory Termination**;
  **Eviction — 24 CFR 982.552(b)(2), 24 CFR 5.2005(c)(1)**: "The PHA must terminate assistance
  whenever a family is evicted... for a serious or repeated violation of the lease."
- PHA Policy: "A family will be considered evicted if the family **moves after a legal eviction order
  has been issued, whether or not physical enforcement... was necessary.**"
- **"This action includes a three-year ban for all adult household members. You may reapply to the
  waiting list but will not be eligible for assistance until June 13, 2029."** ← THE 3-YEAR BAR.
- Right to Informal Hearing; return form by **June 30, 2026, 4:30 PM.**

### Tracey's dispute (DOCUMENTED — the 4 HAJC forms she sent)
- **Informal Hearing Request** (caseworker Christine Sampson): "The dates and information aren't
  correct and I am confused. I dispute that this qualifies as a mandatory termination under
  24 CFR 982.552(b)(2). The court did not find a serious or repeated lease violation."
- Handwritten statement: judgment "was entered pursuant to a mediation agreement in which **no
  restitution judgment was entered** and which only addressed a move-out date, **not a lease
  violation.**" Disputes the **May 22** vacate date (all court docs say May 31).
- Also submitted: Remote Informal Hearing consent + Reasonable Accommodation notice. Signed 6/30/26.

**THE POLICY CORE (the loophole this case exposes):** The mediation agreement entered
**no judgment of restitution and found no lease violation** — it only set a move-out date.
Yet HAJC treated the downstream general judgment as an "eviction for a serious or repeated
lease violation," triggering **mandatory** voucher termination + a **3-year bar**. So a landlord
can convert a no-fault, mediated move-out into a voucher-ending "eviction" on paper, regardless of cause.
